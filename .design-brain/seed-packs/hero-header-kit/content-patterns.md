# Hero Header Kit Content Patterns

- Product launch: category label, promise, proof cue, product visual, demo CTA.
- Service business: audience pain, service promise, trust cue, booking/contact CTA.
- Ecommerce promo: sale badge, product/collection visual, urgency, shop CTA.
- Agency/portfolio: positioning, selected work cue, contact CTA.
- Event/travel: date/location/destination cue, immersive image, booking CTA.

Do not add unrelated downstream sections unless the task asks for a landing page or full website.
