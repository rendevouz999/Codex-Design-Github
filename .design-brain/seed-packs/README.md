# Design Brain Seed Packs

Seed packs are medium-specific planning scaffolds. They do not force a layout, but they make every design start with the right structure, editable areas, design-system scope, and P0/P1/P2 checklist.

Each seed pack contains:

- `layouts.md`
- `checklist.md`
- `content-patterns.md`
- `design-system-scope.md`

Use `scripts/design_brain_module_select.py` to choose the relevant seed pack.
