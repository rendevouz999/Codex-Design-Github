# Website Template Layouts

Use this seed pack when the product is a website template, HTML template, PHP template, agency/business website, service website, or full-site template.

## Core Rule

Do not use restrained styling as a structure-count decision. Quiet visual language can exist, but structure count must come from template purpose, buyer journey, marketplace scope, and end-user task flow.

Before designing, select one Figma website product family from `.design-brain/FIGMA_WEBSITE_TEMPLATE_PRODUCT_TYPES.md`. Landing page is one family, not the default.

Do not carry old task-specific omissions such as desktop-only output, no components, no cover, no preview, or no other devices into new briefs as persistent bans. Choose output surfaces from the current request, product standard, and buyer value.

## Structure Count Standards

- Hero/header kit: 1-4 hero or header variants, plus navigation states and responsive examples. Label it as a kit, not a full website.
- Simple campaign landing page: 6-8 sections only when the brief explicitly asks for a narrow campaign, squeeze page, waitlist page, micro landing page, or one focused conversion asset.
- Standard landing page: 8-12 sections when buyer trust, proof, offer details, and objections matter.
- Full business or agency website template: homepage with 8-12 content blocks plus 8-20 inner pages.
- Service or SaaS website template: homepage with 9-14 content blocks plus service/detail, pricing, case study, resource, contact, and auth/demo pages when relevant.
- Serious service/SaaS/business template: usually 10-14 homepage blocks plus visible page-map/design-system notes, since the primary page must prove product completeness.
- Portfolio or creative agency website: homepage with 8-12 content blocks plus project listing, project detail, about, services, blog/news, and contact pages.
- Ecommerce website: 15-40+ pages or screens including shop, category, product detail, cart, checkout, account, wishlist, auth, support, blog, and error states.
- Dashboard or admin template: 30-80+ screens/components including app shell, dashboards, apps, tables, forms, auth, profile, settings, ecommerce/admin flows, components, and states.
- Blog/editorial template: homepage plus archive/category, article detail, author/profile, search, newsletter, about, contact, and error pages.
- Docs/help center template: docs home, category, article detail, search results, API/reference or changelog, support/contact, and utility states.
- Education/course template: homepage plus course catalog, course detail, lesson preview, instructor, pricing/enroll, student progress/dashboard, FAQ/support, and contact.
- Marketplace/listing template: homepage plus listing index, listing detail, search/filter, map/compare, saved/favorites, inquiry/booking, account/profile, and support.
- Event/travel/hospitality template: homepage plus schedule/amenities, room/ticket/listing detail, booking/reservation, gallery, pricing/availability, policies, contact/location, and utility states.

## Homepage Block Map

For a full website template homepage, choose from this map based on buyer and niche:

- Header/navigation with responsive menu behavior.
- Hero or banner with category, audience, offer, proof cue, and primary CTA.
- Trust band: logos, metrics, ratings, credentials, certifications, or client strip.
- Problem/audience-fit or market context.
- About/company/story or mission block.
- Services/features/capabilities with enough variety to avoid repeated cards only.
- Process/workflow/how-it-works.
- Product/service preview, portfolio, case studies, or gallery.
- Comparison, pricing, packages, plans, or offer details when relevant.
- Team, expert profile, or credibility section when relevant.
- Testimonials/reviews/social proof.
- FAQ or objection handling.
- Blog/news/resources/latest insight.
- Contact form, lead capture, booking, map, or CTA.
- Footer with navigation, contact, legal, social, and trust cues.

For a standard product-ready homepage, do not stop after the first 6-7 roles. Select enough blocks to cover the full buyer journey:

- Discovery: header, hero, proof cue, category/audience signal.
- Understanding: problem/context, benefits, features/services, process or workflow.
- Evaluation: product/service preview, comparison, pricing/package, case study, testimonials, metrics, credentials.
- Conversion: FAQ/objection handling, lead form/booking/contact, final CTA, footer trust.

If a block is removed, document which buyer question no longer needs to be answered. Silence is not a design reason.

## Inner Page Map

For full website templates, plan inner pages before visuals:

- About page.
- Service listing and service detail.
- Portfolio/project listing and project detail.
- Blog/news listing and blog/news detail.
- Team listing and team detail when relevant.
- Pricing/package page when relevant.
- FAQ page when objections matter.
- Contact page with form and details.
- Shop/category/product/cart/checkout/account pages for ecommerce.
- Auth pages only when the product needs an account flow.
- 404/error and coming soon when marketplace package completeness matters.

## Evidence From Local Corpus

- Business/finance HTML template includes many home variants and 30+ page files per language direction.
- Minifolio and Monas-style templates use roughly 10-12 homepage sections plus inner pages.
- Ekobyte PHP template uses 10 home partials per homepage variant and 14 top-level PHP pages.
- Medcity uses 12 homepage sections and 26 content pages.
- Maxton ecommerce includes 40+ ecommerce pages and account/auth/support flows.
- Admin/dashboard package includes 70+ HTML screens in one layout mode, plus RTL and alternate navigation variants.
