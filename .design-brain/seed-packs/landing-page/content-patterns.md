# Landing Page Content Patterns

- Promise: clear outcome in one sentence.
- Proof: real metric, logo, quote, screenshot, case cue, or placeholder label.
- Feature: action-oriented benefit plus concrete evidence.
- CTA: describe what happens next.
- Objection handling: FAQ, comparison, risk reducer, guarantee, detail, or support cue when buyer confidence needs it.
- Scope note: landing pages can be focused, but focus does not mean under-structured. Remove only sections that the specific conversion goal truly does not need.
