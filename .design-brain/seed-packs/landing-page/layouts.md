# Landing Page Layouts

Landing page is a focused conversion page, not the default standard for every website template. If the buyer asks for a website template, full-site template, HTML template, PHP template, ecommerce template, dashboard, blog, or portfolio site, route to the matching medium first.

## Structure Count Standards

- Narrow campaign landing page: 6-8 sections only when the brief explicitly asks for narrow campaign, squeeze, waitlist, or micro landing scope.
- Standard product/service landing page: 8-12 sections.
- Long-form sales or trust-heavy service page: 10-14 sections when proof, comparison, objections, and decision support are required.

Do not reduce section count because the visual mood is clean, modern, or restrained. Visual simplicity is allowed; structural incompleteness is not.

- Hero: category signal, promise, primary CTA, product/service evidence.
- Proof band: logos, metrics, testimonial, or concrete trust cue.
- Value modules: benefits with varied rhythm, not repeated cards only.
- Mechanism/process: how the product/service works.
- Offer or package/pricing section when relevant.
- Objection handling: FAQ, guarantees, support, security, logistics, or comparison.
- Final CTA and footer: conversion closure and trust.

If the page is meant to be sold as a template product rather than used for a single small campaign, use at least 8 meaningful sections and prefer the standard or long-form ranges. A marketplace buyer expects adaptable modules, not only a nice short conversion concept.
