# Form Validation Craft

- Every form field needs label, helper/error slot, focus state, disabled state, and submission feedback.
- Error messages should describe the fix, not merely the failure.
- Keep visual state and accessibility state in sync.
- Multi-step forms need progress, back/next behavior, and save/exit expectations.
- Newsletter/email capture forms should remain compact and clear.
- Checkout or lead-gen forms must minimize friction and make trust cues visible.
