# Anti-AI-Slop Craft

- Avoid default purple/blue trust gradients unless the brand specifically needs them.
- Avoid vague feature cards with generic icons and filler copy.
- Avoid invented metrics such as "10x faster" or "99.9% uptime" without source or placeholder label.
- Avoid decorative blobs, random bento grids, and repeated CTA bands with no content progression.
- Avoid one-note dark blue, beige, purple, or espresso palettes.
- Replace generic CTAs with action-specific language.
- Add at least one product-specific proof detail that could not belong to every template.
- Avoid using visual restraint as a shortcut for missing required sections, pages, screens, states, or buyer-journey steps.
- For website templates, classify the product scope before reducing structure: hero/header kit, landing page, full website, ecommerce, dashboard/admin, portfolio, blog/editorial, or service site.
