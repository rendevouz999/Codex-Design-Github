# Design Brain Craft Axis

Craft modules are universal design rules that sit between medium playbooks and style packs. A medium decides what must exist; a direction pack decides mood; craft modules decide whether the work feels professionally made.

Craft modules are non-override support. They must follow `.design-brain/DESIGN_CRAFT_NON_OVERRIDE_CONTRACT.md`: Brain Design routing, cognition, simulation, lifecycle, design-system, post-build, and completion receipt gates win over craft/style advice.

Use `scripts/design_brain_module_select.py` to choose the right craft modules for a task. Do not load every craft file by default.
