# Layout Composition Craft

- Choose one primary hierarchy anchor per screen, slide, or frame.
- Change composition when changing content role: proof, feature, pricing, table, story, or CTA should not all look alike.
- Use asymmetry only with clear alignment logic.
- Avoid cards inside decorative cards.
- Prefer inspectable product surfaces over atmospheric mockups.
- Reinterpret references by changing first structural move, grouping, media strategy, and rhythm.
- If a layout could fit any startup with copy swapped, add buyer-specific evidence.
- Structural completeness comes before visual reduction: do not remove required sections or pages to make the template feel quieter.
- Use clean composition to organize complete structure, not to avoid it.
