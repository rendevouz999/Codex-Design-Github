# Color Craft

- Assign roles: background, surface, text, muted, border, primary accent, secondary accent, status colors.
- Accent should have a job: CTA, selected state, data highlight, or brand signal.
- Avoid one-note palettes where every element is the same hue family.
- Do not use trendy gradients unless the medium and buyer benefit from them.
- Check contrast for text, controls, chart labels, and slide thumbnails.
- For dashboards, reserve saturated colors for states and important data.
- For marketplace previews, color should frame the product without hiding it.
