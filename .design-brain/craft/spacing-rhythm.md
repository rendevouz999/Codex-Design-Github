# Spacing Rhythm Craft

- Use a consistent spacing scale; avoid one-off gaps.
- Alternate density: open section, proof/detail section, compact module, breathing pause.
- Do not repeat identical card grids across consecutive sections.
- Use tighter spacing inside operational tools and broader spacing in editorial or brand work.
- Keep related elements closer than unrelated elements.
- For decks, safe margins matter more than page-scroll whitespace.
- For Figma templates, spacing should survive auto-layout edits.
