# Advanced Style Grammar Matrix

Use this file to convert style words into concrete design rules. Never stop at "premium", "clean", or "modern".

## Style Grammar Fields

For every selected style direction, define:

- Typography behavior: scale, weight, pairing, line length, casing.
- Spacing behavior: compact, spacious, editorial, dense, operational.
- Composition behavior: centered, split, asymmetrical, modular, timeline, gallery, app-shell.
- Surface behavior: flat, bordered, glass, elevated, editorial paper, data panel.
- Motif behavior: line, shape, crop, grid, icon, number, divider, image mask.
- Imagery behavior: real product, editorial photo, screenshot, abstract texture, portrait, none.
- Color behavior: accent role, background role, semantic roles, chart roles, contrast.
- Color relationship test: whether A+B, A+B+C, or a richer A-G role map works together for category, hierarchy, CTA emphasis, accessibility, and batch differentiation.
- Effect/gradient behavior: where depth, lighting, shadow, glow, overlay, texture, or gradient appears and what role it performs.
- Medium fit: website, deck, web deck, dashboard, brand, UI kit, preview.
- Anti-pattern: what this style must not become.

## Core Style Grammars

### Premium Clean

- Typography: restrained scale, high legibility, confident headings, no excessive weights.
- Spacing: generous but purposeful.
- Composition: clear grid with one memorable break in rhythm.
- Surface: subtle borders or quiet elevation.
- Motif: restrained accent lines, precise crops, high-quality imagery.
- Anti-pattern: generic white cards with soft shadows everywhere.

### Editorial Refined

- Typography: stronger display type, careful body measure, high contrast between title and text.
- Spacing: large pauses, asymmetrical balance, fewer modules.
- Composition: magazine-like pacing, image/text tension.
- Surface: paper-like or flat surfaces.
- Motif: rule lines, captions, numbered sections, curated crops.
- Anti-pattern: decorative serif use without content hierarchy.

### Operational Dashboard

- Typography: compact, readable, hierarchy for scanning.
- Spacing: dense but organized.
- Composition: app shell, panels, tables, chart grids, comparison rows.
- Surface: controlled panels with state colors.
- Motif: status chips, trend lines, filter controls, chart accents.
- Anti-pattern: marketing hero logic or oversized decorative cards.

### Presentation Storytelling

- Typography: large slide titles, short body, clear emphasis.
- Spacing: safe margins and strong whitespace.
- Composition: slide roles, section dividers, proof slides, visual reveals.
- Surface: theme backgrounds, image masks, chart/table slides, callouts.
- Motif: recurring slide number, divider, title lockup, highlight shape.
- Anti-pattern: landing page sections copied into slides.

### Motion Stage Deck

- Typography: stage-sized display titles, short supporting lines, one emphasis weight per slide.
- Spacing: fixed stage padding, repeated block rhythm, no reflow at smaller viewports.
- Composition: slide roles on a fixed 16:9 stage, staged reveals, chrome outside the stage.
- Surface: flat stage backgrounds, quiet panels, image masks that survive uniform scaling.
- Motif: progress indicator, slide number, recurring accent shape, one reveal direction per role.
- Anti-pattern: motion standing in for structure, so the deck reads as an effect reel.

### Marketplace Perspective

- Typography: compact product naming and clear category labels.
- Spacing: enough room for previews to remain inspectable.
- Composition: cover, preview wall, detail crop, responsive spread.
- Surface: staged background only when product remains readable.
- Motif: frame shadows, device frames, callout labels, product badges.
- Anti-pattern: dramatic mockup angles that hide design quality.
